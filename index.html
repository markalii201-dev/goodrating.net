What We Remove

Reviews That Break Google's Rules

Google does not allow fake, spam, off topic or conflict of interest reviews. When one lands on your profile, we work to get it taken down.

Fake Reviews

Reviews from people who never hired you, never visited, and never paid you.

Competitor Attacks

Rival businesses leaving one star reviews to pull your rating down on purpose.

Spam And Bots

Automated junk, repeated text, and accounts that post the same review everywhere.

Off Topic

Rants about politics, the news, or anything that has nothing to do with your business.

Conflict Of Interest

Reviews from former staff, rivals, or anyone with a stake in hurting your business.

Abuse And Private Info

Harassment, hate, threats, and reviews that share private details about you or your team.

Straight answer first: a genuine review from a real customer that follows Google's rules is not something we promise to remove. We check every link and tell you which ones qualify.
